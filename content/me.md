# Eric

## Work

I'm a software developer based in Miami, FL interested in data science, computer
graphics, and math visualization. If any of that sounds interesting to you,
check out my [resume](/resume) and some of my [projects](/projects).

## Uses This

My daily driver is a Lenovo IdeaPad Flex 5 with Arch Linux, which I've
been on since late-2022. I'm currently on the niri compositor but have used
Hyprland and awesome in the past. Most of my desktop wallpapers come from
[/wg](https://boards.4chan.org/wg/). I read and take notes in my Boox Go 10.3
and organize my writing in Obsidian using a zettelkasten note system. I'm a fan
of the fonts Terminus, Tamzen, Vollkorn, Libre Baskerville, and Kerkis.  I edit
photos with Photopea and use Aseprite for pixel art.
