Something that's always perplexed me is how [heating up magnets](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RI3QHA5k8w4) causes them to lose their magnetism.
On the surface, it's strange how this innate property can be entirely negated. I mean, magnetism is such a pervasive part of everyday life yet exerts itself so invisibly.
Most people's notion of a magnet stops at "opposites attract and likes repel", but there's so much more to them that meets the eye.

# The Ising Model

The [Ising Model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ising_model) is one of the simplest models of ferromagnetism in statistical mechanics.
