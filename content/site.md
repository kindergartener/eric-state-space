# Name and Origins

A [state space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State-space_representation) is
a mathematical model that captures every way in which a system can exist. This
site strives to be exactly that: an experiment of self-documentation on the
internet. My hope is that in writing down my ideas and creations, I come to
better understand the person that is Eric, and maybe you can too.

# Design

This website takes visual inspiration from
[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia), 2000s Japanese
[FC2 blogs](https://blog.fc2.com/en/), [Gwern.net](https://gwern.net/),
[neocities](https://neocities.org/), [LessWrong](https://www.lesswrong.com/),
and [prof-g's site](https://www2.math.upenn.edu/~ghrist/). I hold no credit over
the design of this site and anything stolen was done so in the name of great art.
